# Home Energy 3D - Home Assistant Add-on

Interactive 3D Home Energy Monitor for Solax Solar, Octopus Energy, myenergi Zappi & Peugeot e-2008.

## Installation
1. In Home Assistant, go to **Settings > Add-ons > Add-on Store**.
2. Click the 3 dots in the top right and select **Repositories**.
3. Add `https://github.com/aaronwatt91/ha-energy-3d-card`.
4. Find **Home Energy 3D** in the store and click **Install**.
5. Enable **Show in sidebar** and click **Start**.
